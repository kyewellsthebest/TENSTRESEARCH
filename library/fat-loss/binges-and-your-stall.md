# One big day a week, and Kye's stall

**Verdict:** confidence **moderate**; last updated 2026-11-08.

- A +1,000 kcal day adds at most about 100–125 g of fat. It cancels part of the week's deficit; it doesn't wreck it.
- Monday's heavier weight is glycogen, water, salt and gut contents, gone in 2–5 days. Judge progress Friday to
  Friday, or on 7-day averages.
- **The stall:** a flat weight means your weekly average intake equals maintenance. The likely causes are
  under-logging and weekend "maintenance" days that are really surpluses. Weigh food and log weekends.
