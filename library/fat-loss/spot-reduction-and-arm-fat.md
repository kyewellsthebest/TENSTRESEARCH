# Spot reduction and arm fat

**Verdict:** spot reduction: confidence **strong** that it doesn't work for limbs; arm-fat reading: **moderate**.
Last updated 2026-11-04.

- **Spot reduction:**
  - A meta-analysis of 13 studies with 1,158 people found no effect.
  - Training one arm for 12 weeks took fat off both arms equally on MRI.
- **Why your arms may read fatter:**
  - Smart scales and InBody are unreliable for limbs.
  - The same 6 mm of fat is about 18% of an arm but only about 9% of a waist.
  - In men, belly fat hides inside the abdomen.
  - In 852 young men measured by DXA, the arm was the *leanest* region.
- **How to check:** DXA scan fasted, regional fat read in kg, plus skinfolds in mm.
