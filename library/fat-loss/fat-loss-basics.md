# Fat loss basics for a trained lifter

**Verdict:** confidence **strong** on energy balance, **moderate** on the details; last updated 2026-11-04.

- **The deficit drives fat loss.** At equal calories, low-carb and low-fat barely differ.
- **You burn fat and carbs at the same time, all day.** Glycogen doesn't have to run out first. In a metabolic ward,
  low-carb raised fat burning but lost *less* body fat.
- **Fasted cardio** burns about 3 g more fat per session, and gives the same fat loss over weeks.
- **Carb cycling / being "flat"** is glycogen plus water. It burns no extra fat at the same weekly calories.
- **The plan:**
  - Lose about 0.5% of body weight a week.
  - Eat 2.0–2.4 g/kg of protein.
  - Keep lifting heavy.
  - Put carbs around training.
  - Don't eat back exercise calories.
  - Sleep 7–9 h.
