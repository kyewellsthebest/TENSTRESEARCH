# Peptides: 48 substances ranked

**Verdict:** last updated 2026-11-04.

- **Most researched:** semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide. Their strong benefits are in obesity, not lean
  lifters, and 25–40% of the weight lost is lean tissue.
- **Muscle in healthy people:** nothing here has been shown to build muscle or strength. GH adds about 2 kg of lean
  mass with no strength gain; MK-677 adds about 1 kg, also with no strength gain.
- **BPC-157:** no controlled human trial. **TB-500:** zero human studies.
- **Unknown safety ranks worst.** Grey-market products have tested at 5–75% purity and contained arsenic, lead and
  endotoxin.
- **Law:** most are prescription-only in Australia (TGA) and banned in sport (WADA).
