# Beta-alanine, citrulline, beetroot nitrate, sodium bicarbonate

**Verdict:** these give small same-day boosts to reps in acidic, short-rest efforts. None improves 1RM, and none has
shown extra muscle in trained lifters. All are second-tier behind creatine and caffeine. Confidence **Moderate**
(acute effect); **Very limited** (long-term). Last updated 2026-11-15, audited.

## What to do
- **Citrulline: skip it.** It's the weakest of the four, and the good trials in trained lifters were null.
- **Beta-alanine: optional, only for lots of short-rest, high-rep or density work.** Take 3.2–6.4 g/day in split
  doses for at least 4 weeks; tingling is harmless. Don't expect anything on straight 6–12-rep sets with 2–3 min rest.
- **Beetroot: optional and cheap to test.** Take one or two 70 mL concentrated shots (about 6–13 mmol nitrate) 2–3 h
  before a key session, and skip antibacterial mouthwash. Expect about 1 extra rep across several sets at most.
- **Bicarbonate: not for routine training.** At 0.3 g/kg (about 26 g bicarbonate, about 7 g sodium) the GI risk is real.
  Keep it for a 30 s–10 min test or event, trialled first. Avoid it with high blood pressure or kidney issues.

## Key numbers
| Supplement | Finding (95% CI) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Beta-alanine, overall | ES 0.18 (0.08 to 0.28) | Saunders 2017 |
| Beta-alanine, trained men, 0.5–10 min efforts | ES 0.39 (0.09 to 0.69) | Georgiou 2024 |
| Beta-alanine, fat-free mass | +0.05 kg (−0.71 to 0.82) | Ashtary-Larky 2022 |
| Beta-alanine, tingling | OR 8.9 (2.2 to 32.6) | Dolan 2019 |
| Citrulline, strength/power | SMD 0.20 (0.01 to 0.39); about +3 reps out of about 51 across 5 sets | Trexler 2019; Vårvik 2021 |
| Beetroot, squat/bench reps to failure | SMD 0.43 (0.16 to 0.69); only 5–6 small studies, mostly one product | Tan 2023 |
| Bicarbonate, muscular endurance | SMD 0.37 (0.15 to 0.59); strength 0.02 (−0.09 to 0.13) | Grgic 2020 |

## Caveats
- **Beta-alanine:** Natural Alternatives International (CarnoSyn) has funded or supplied much of the research.
- **Pouresmaeili 2026:** reported big quad-CSA gains with beta-alanine plus bicarbonate, but it is single-blind with
  10 per group, and the placebo group's gain was +5–9%, not +5%. Not credible until replicated.
- **Citrulline:** the "+53% reps" result (Pérez-Guisado 2010) covers the final set only and has never been reproduced.
- **AIS ratings:** Group A for beta-alanine, nitrate and bicarbonate is confirmed via web search only. Citrulline's
  group is unverified.
