# Caffeine and pre-workouts

**Verdict:** caffeine reliably gives a small boost to a session, but nothing shows it builds extra muscle. Pre-workouts
add little beyond their caffeine and carry contamination risk. Confidence **Strong** (acute effect); **Limited**
(long-term, genotype). Last updated 2026-11-15, audited.

## What to do
- Take **2–3 mg/kg (about 175–260 mg)** 45–60 min before your hardest sessions only. Keep the whole day **≤400 mg**.
  EFSA's single-dose figure is 200 mg; 260 mg sits above it, in line with EFSA's per-kg basis.
- Have your **last caffeine ≥8–10 h before bed**. For evening sessions, use none or ≤1 mg/kg (about 90 mg). The sleep
  loss probably costs you more than the 1–2% gain.
- **Skip branded pre-workouts.** Plain caffeine (plus your daily creatine) gives all of the proven effect. If you use
  one anyway, pick a fully disclosed label with no proprietary blend, tested by Informed Sport or HASTA, and avoid
  DMAA, DMHA/octodrine, BMPEA and "geranium extract".
- Daily use builds partial tolerance within about 2–3 weeks, so save it for the days that matter.
- Don't buy CYP1A2 genotype tests.

## Key numbers
| Outcome | Finding (95% CI) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1RM strength | SMD 0.20 (0.03 to 0.36), about +1–2% | Grgic 2018 |
| Strength, umbrella of 9 meta-analyses | SMD 0.18 (0.14 to 0.21) | Bilondi 2024 |
| Reps to failure | SMD 0.30 (0.21 to 0.38); bench about +0.9 reps (0.33 to 1.41) | Bilondi 2024; Ferreira 2021 |
| Bar velocity / power | SMD 0.42 (0.19 to 0.65), I² 85%; power 0.21 (0.12 to 0.30) | Xiao 2025 |
| Low dose 0.9–2 mg/kg | Strength d 0.17, endurance d 0.21 | Grgic 2022 |
| Sleep | Total sleep −45 min, efficiency −7%, deep sleep −11 min | Gardiner 2023 |
| Long-term bench 1RM, 4 weeks | +13.5% vs +11.3% with placebo; no difference | Giráldez-Costas 2021 |
| Pre-workout labels | Average 254 mg caffeine per serving; 44% of ingredients in undisclosed proprietary blends | Jagim 2019 |
| Contamination | 9 prohibited stimulants found in supplements | Cohen 2021 |

## Caveats
- The whole-session volume gain is unproven (Filip-Stachnik 2023, n = 10, habituated users: 185 vs 180 reps).
- The CYP1A2 "CC slow metaboliser" result rests on only 34 people.
- Gardiner's "13.2 h before bed for a pre-workout" figure is a model extrapolation.
- The ISSN position-stand authors have supplement and genetic-testing ties. The one positive lean-mass pre-workout
  trial was manufacturer-funded and confounded.
