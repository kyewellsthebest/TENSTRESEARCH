# The five biggest muscle-growth factors (trained lifters)

**Verdict:** confidence **moderate**; last updated 2026-11-03.

The factors, in order of importance:
1. **Effort** (tied first) — end most sets 1–3 reps short of failure; go closer with light weights.
2. **Weekly hard-set volume** (tied first) — more hard sets help, with diminishing returns. About 10–20 sets per
   muscle per week is a sensible working range.
3. **Exercise selection and muscle length** — avoid doing only short top-half reps; load muscles in the stretch.
4. **Rest** — enough to keep your reps: at least 90 s, 2–3 min on heavy compound lifts.
5. **Progressive overload** — keep sets hard as you get stronger. Adding weight and adding reps work equally well.

**No effect in trained lifters:** tempo (anywhere from 0.5 to 8 s per rep), pauses, squeezes, eccentric emphasis,
drop sets / supersets / rest-pause, and training frequency once volume is equal.
