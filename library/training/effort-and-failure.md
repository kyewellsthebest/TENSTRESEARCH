# Proximity to failure and tracking effort

**Verdict:** confidence **moderate**; last updated 2026-11-03.

- Effort is the gate. A set far from failure barely counts.
- Going all the way to failure adds little over stopping 1–3 reps short for trained lifters, but costs more fatigue.
- Light loads need to go closer to failure than heavy ones.
- Lifters guess their reps in reserve to within about 1 rep near failure, but badly far from it. Coaches were 4.8
  reps off a third of the way into a set. Long, light sets are where a sensor could genuinely help.
