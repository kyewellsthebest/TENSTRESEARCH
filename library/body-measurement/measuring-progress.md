# Measuring fat and progress

**Verdict:** confidence **moderate**; last updated 2026-11-04.

- **Daily morning weight:** compare 7-day averages. Water and glycogen swings are bigger than a week of fat loss.
- **Waist at the navel:** measure weekly.
- **Skinfolds or ultrasound:** every 2–4 weeks, by the same tester.
- **DXA:** every 8–12 weeks, fasted, with no training that day. Arm fat is its least precise region.
- **Smart scales / InBody:** good for body weight; individual fat-mass error is a median 2–4 kg, and limb readings
  are the least trustworthy.
