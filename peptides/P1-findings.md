# P1 — Metabolic / fat-loss peptides: findings

Agent P1. Searched 2026-11-04. No dosing, cycling, reconstitution, injection or sourcing information for any
substance; trial doses of approved drugs appear only to identify which arm a result came from.
Access note: every figure below was reached through abstracts, regulator pages, sponsor releases or trade-press
summaries of the primary paper. `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov` could not be reached from this environment (DNS refused),
so **no full text was read**. Nothing in this file is FULL access. Items marked UNVERIFIED are recalled, not found
this session.

## 10-line summary

1. The incretin drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide) are by a wide margin the best-researched peptides in this whole project: tens of thousands of people in phase-3 RCTs, hard cardiovascular outcomes, regulator approvals.
2. Weight loss, placebo-subtracted, in people with obesity: liraglutide about 5 points, semaglutide 2.4 mg about 12, tirzepatide about 15–18, semaglutide 7.2 mg about 18; retatrutide (not approved) 24–28% raw in phase 3 toplines.
3. Semaglutide is the only one with a positive placebo-controlled CV outcome trial in people without diabetes (SELECT: MACE HR 0.80, 0.72–0.90).
4. Roughly a quarter to a third of the weight lost is lean mass (tirzepatide DXA ~25%, CagriSema DXA ~33%, STEP-1 DXA ~39% by one secondary calculation) — about 5–8 kg of lean tissue in people who started obese.
5. Stop the drug and most of the weight comes back: STEP-1 regained ~two-thirds within a year; SURMOUNT-4 regained ~14% of body weight on switch to placebo.
6. GI side effects are the rule, not the exception (CagriSema 80% vs 40% placebo); discontinuation for adverse events roughly doubles vs placebo (SELECT 16.6% vs 8.2%).
7. Real but rare harms: gallbladder disease (RR 1.37 overall, 2.29 in weight-loss trials), NAION (EMA: "very rare", ~1 extra case per 10,000 person-years); pancreatitis signal weak in RCTs; FDA found no suicidality link and asked for the warning to be removed (Jan 2026).
8. Grey-market and compounded product is a separate and worse risk: test-bought semaglutide at 7–14% purity with endotoxin in one sample; FDA reports of 5–20x overdoses from compounded vials; Australia banned GLP-1 compounding from 1 Oct 2024; illicit retatrutide tied to 14 FAERS hospitalisations.
9. AOD-9604 failed its obesity programme; MOTS-c has no published human efficacy trial of the native peptide — only a sponsor-reported 4-week phase 1b of an analogue that did not beat placebo on liver fat.
10. For a lean, resistance-trained man there is **no trial evidence of benefit** from any of these; every trial enrolled people with obesity, and the lean-mass cost per kilo lost is expected to be worse, not better, when there is little fat to lose (inference, not measured).

## Semaglutide (Wegovy / Ozempic / Rybelsus; Wegovy HD 7.2 mg)

**Evidence level:** human RCTs, phase 3, multiple approvals. Most-researched substance in P1.

**Demonstrated benefits**
- STEP-1 (Wilding, NEJM 2021; n=1,961 adults, BMI ≥30 or ≥27+comorbidity, no diabetes; 68 wk; 2.4 mg weekly vs placebo, both with lifestyle): body weight −14.9% vs −2.4% (difference ≈ −12.4 points). [ABSTRACT/SECONDARY] Novo Nordisk funded.
- STEP UP (n=1,407; 5:1:1 to 7.2 mg, 2.4 mg, placebo; 72 wk): −20.7% / −17.5% / −2.4% (efficacy estimand); 18.7% for 7.2 mg under treatment-regimen estimand. FDA approved Wegovy HD 19 Mar 2026. No peer-reviewed paper found; figures from sponsor release and trade press. [SECONDARY]
- SELECT (Lincoff, NEJM 2023; n=17,604 with established CVD and BMI ≥27, no diabetes; mean follow-up 39.8 months): MACE 6.5% vs 8.0%, HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.90). CV death alone HR 0.85 (0.71–1.01, not significant). All-cause death HR 0.81 (0.71–0.93) — secondary. [ABSTRACT/SECONDARY]

**Body composition (lean mass)**
- STEP-1 DXA subgroup (n=140; 95 sema / 45 placebo; exploratory, BMI ≤40 only; presented ENDO 2021, not in the NEJM paper): lean mass −9.7% from baseline; ClinicalTrials.gov reports −5.8 ± 4.6 kg lean (n=83); fat −19.3% (poster); lean proportion of body mass rose 3.0 points. A secondary source computes lean as ~39% of weight lost — this is a calculation by a third party, not a trial-reported figure. [SECONDARY]

**Adverse events and discontinuation**
- SELECT: AEs leading to discontinuation 16.6% vs 8.2%; GI-driven discontinuation 10.0% vs 2.0% (ACC summary). [SECONDARY]
- STEP UP: dysaesthesia (altered skin sensation) 22% at 7.2 mg vs 6% at 2.4 mg vs 0.3% placebo — new dose-dependent signal. Serious AEs 6.8% / 10.9% / 5.5%. [SECONDARY]
- STEP-1 GI AE rate (~74% vs ~48%) and AE discontinuation (~7% vs ~3%): UNVERIFIED — recalled, not reached this session.
- Gallbladder, pancreatitis, NAION, suicidality: see the class section below.

**Weight regain:** STEP-1 extension (Wilding, Diabetes Obes Metab 2022; n=327 followed a year after stopping): regained 11.6 (SD 7.7) points vs 1.9 placebo; net loss at wk 120 5.6% vs 0.1%; ≈ two-thirds of the loss regained; cardiometabolic gains reverted towards baseline. All exploratory. [ABSTRACT]

**Regulatory:** FDA-approved (obesity, CV risk reduction, T2D). TGA: Schedule 4 prescription-only, ARTG-registered (Ozempic, Wegovy); PBAC recommended an authority PBS listing of Wegovy for established CVD with obesity, listing date unconfirmed as of the latest source (2026). WADA: not prohibited; on the Monitoring Program since 2024 (secondary). FDA shortage-era compounding has ended; Australia removed all GLP-1 RAs from the pharmacist compounding exemption from 1 Oct 2024.

**Unknowns:** lifelong-use outcomes beyond ~4 years; muscle *function* (strength, quality) as opposed to DXA lean mass; effects in lean people.

## Tirzepatide (Mounjaro / Zepbound)

**Evidence level:** human RCTs, phase 3, approved.

- SURMOUNT-1 (Jastreboff, NEJM 2022; n=2,539, obesity/overweight without diabetes; 72 wk; 5/10/15 mg vs placebo): −15.0 / −19.5 / −20.9% vs placebo (placebo value ≈ −3% UNVERIFIED this session). ≥25% loss: 15.3/32.3/36.2% vs 1.5%. [ABSTRACT/SECONDARY] Lilly funded.
- SURMOUNT-1 DXA substudy (Diabetes Obes Metab 2025, doi 10.1111/dom.16275; n=160, 124 tirzepatide pooled / 36 placebo): weight −21.3% vs −5.3%; fat mass −33.9% vs −8.2%; lean mass −10.9% vs −2.6%; ≈75% fat / 25% lean of weight lost in *both* arms; ≈5.6 kg lean lost vs 1.2 kg (secondary). Subgroups post hoc; high risk of bias from missing DXA data. [ABSTRACT]
- SURMOUNT-5 (Aronne, NEJM 2025, doi 10.1056/NEJMoa2416394; n=751; open-label; 72 wk; max-tolerated tirzepatide vs semaglutide): −20.2% vs −13.7%; waist −18.4 vs −13.0 cm; GI-AE discontinuation 2.7% vs 5.6%. Men lost about 6 points less than women in both arms. Lilly funded; open label. [SECONDARY]
- SURMOUNT-4 (Aronne, JAMA 2024; 36-wk open-label lead-in −20.9%, then n=670 randomised to continue or placebo for 52 wk): placebo arm regained ~14%; continuing arm lost a further ~5.5%; wk 0–88 −25.3% vs −9.9%. [ABSTRACT/SECONDARY]
- SURPASS-CVOT (NEJM 2025; T2D + ASCVD; vs dulaglutide, no placebo): MACE 12.2% vs 13.1%, HR 0.92 (95.3% CI 0.83–1.01) — non-inferior, not superior. All-cause death HR 0.84 (secondary, not multiplicity-controlled). [SECONDARY]
- A 2026 analysis reported tirzepatide users lost more muscle than semaglutide users; not peer reviewed, not used. [SECONDARY, unreviewed]

**Regulatory:** FDA-approved (obesity, OSA, T2D). TGA: S4, ARTG (Mounjaro) — Lilly declined the PBS price for T2D; obesity listing not recommended (PBAC Nov 2025, secondary). WADA: not prohibited; Monitoring Program from 1 Jan 2026 (secondary).

## Liraglutide (Saxenda / Victoza)

- SCALE Obesity and Prediabetes (Pi-Sunyer, NEJM 2015; n=3,731, 2,487 lira / 1,244 placebo; 56 wk; 3.0 mg daily): −8.0% vs −2.6%. Gallbladder AEs 2.5% vs 1.0%; pancreatitis 0.3% vs 0.1% (or as 2.7 vs 1.0 and 0.3 vs 0.1 per 100 patient-years). [ABSTRACT/SECONDARY] Novo funded.
- Weakest of the approved incretins; daily injection; now generic in several markets (UNVERIFIED for AU).
- FDA, TGA S4 approved. WADA not prohibited.

## Retatrutide (GIP/GLP-1/glucagon triple agonist) — NOT APPROVED anywhere

- Phase 2 (Jastreboff, NEJM 2023; n=338; 48 wk): −8.7 / −17.1 / −22.8 / −24.2% across the four dose groups vs −2.1% placebo at 48 wk (secondary endpoint; primary was 24 wk, −17.5% at top dose). [ABSTRACT]
- TRIUMPH-4 (topline Dec 2025; obesity + knee OA; 68 wk; n=445 per Lilly, 405 per one memo): −26.4% (9 mg) / −28.7% (12 mg) vs −2.1%; WOMAC pain −75.8% at best. **Dysaesthesia 8.8% / 20.9% vs 0.7%.** AE discontinuation 12.2/18.2% vs 4.0% in one source, 9/12% vs 5% in another — unresolved. [SECONDARY, sponsor topline]
- TRIUMPH-1 (topline May 2026; n=2,339 without diabetes; 80 wk): up to −28.3% (Lilly) / −30.3% (AJMC — different estimand). TRIUMPH-2 (n=1,152 T2D): −12.7/−19.1/−20.8%. TRIUMPH-3 (n=1,949, BMI ≥35 + CVD): up to −22.6%; AE discontinuation 9.8/13.5% vs 4.8%. None peer reviewed yet. US BLA planned Q1 2027. [SECONDARY]
- No body-composition data found. No CV outcome data.
- **Grey market:** sold as "research use only". FDA has issued 14 (Public Citizen) to 21 (another review) warning letters to sellers since late 2024; 14 FAERS hospitalisations by June 2026 (Public Citizen); Lilly sued six sellers (Aug 2026). A Class II recall of one vendor's product reported secondhand only. [SECONDARY]
- WADA: not named; on the text of S0 (substances under clinical development with no approval) it is prohibited — inference from S0 wording, not checked against the 2026 list text.
- TGA: not on ARTG. Scheduling not verified this session.

## Cagrilintide / CagriSema (amylin analogue ± semaglutide) — NOT APPROVED (filing status not verified)

- REDEFINE 1 (Garvey, NEJM 2025, doi 10.1056/NEJMoa2502081; 68 wk; CagriSema 2.4/2.4 vs sema alone vs cagri alone vs placebo): −20.4% vs −3.0% placebo (treatment-regimen); −22.7% if adherent. Semaglutide alone −14.9%, cagrilintide alone −11.5% (secondary). GI AEs 79.6% vs 39.9%; AE discontinuation ~6% vs 3.7%. Novo funded. Sema-alone arm (−14.9%) is the comparator to note: the combination added ~5.5 points. [ABSTRACT/SECONDARY]
- REDEFINE 1 DXA (ECO 2026; n=252): 66.9% of loss fat / **33.1% lean soft tissue**; fat −17.0 kg vs −3.4; lean −8.4 kg vs −2.6. [SECONDARY, conference]
- Cagrilintide as monotherapy is the one an amylin-curious lifter would hear about: −11.5% in people with obesity; no approval. WADA: S0 by inference.

## Survodutide (GLP-1/glucagon dual agonist, Boehringer) — NOT APPROVED

- SYNCHRONIZE-1 (n=725 without T2D; 76 wk): up to −16.6% vs −3.2% (efficacy estimand); placebo-adjusted ~13.4% per one analyst. Published NEJM/Nat Med June 2026 (not read). Visceral fat −34%, liver fat −63% in substudy. SYNCHRONIZE-2 (T2D) up to −13.1%. Filing planned 2027 (unconfirmed). [SECONDARY]
- AE rates not obtained this session.

## Orforglipron (Foundayo) — oral, **non-peptide** small-molecule GLP-1 agonist

- Included only for completeness; it is not a peptide. ATTAIN-1: −12.4% vs −0.9% (adherent, top dose, 72 wk); −11.1% vs −2.1% all randomised. FDA approved 1 Apr 2026 (approved doses lower than the top trial dose). [SECONDARY]

## AOD-9604 (hGH fragment 177–191 analogue)

**Evidence level:** human RCTs exist, and they failed.
- Company (Metabolic Pharmaceuticals, ASX) reported a "successful" 12-wk phase 2b in ~300 people in 2004 (press release only). A later, larger 24-wk phase 2b (536 subjects per secondary sources citing a 2013 review) failed to produce significant weight loss and the programme was terminated in 2007. The two descriptions do not reconcile and the primary data are unpublished; treat effect size as **none demonstrated**. [SECONDARY, sponsor]
- Safety in those trials was described by the company as clean (mild abdominal discomfort at the highest dose) — sponsor claim only. FDA's compounding safety summary flagged immunogenicity risk and serious AEs "possibly associated, causality unclear".
- **Regulatory:** not FDA-approved. FDA's 503A category: removed from Category 2 on 27 Sep 2024 because nominations were withdrawn — which also means it is not on Category 1 and not lawfully compoundable; PCAC reportedly voted against it in Dec 2024 (secondary). TGA: not on ARTG; S4 claim appears only on commercial sites — unverified. "GRAS" claims on vendor sites are a US food-ingredient term and do not establish drug safety. WADA: prohibited at all times; whether under S2 (named GH fragment) or S0 conflicted between secondary sources — official list not reached.
- **Grey market:** no independent purity testing found.

## MOTS-c (mitochondrial-derived peptide)

**Evidence level:** animal + one human physiology study + one sponsor-reported phase 1 of an analogue. No published human RCT of MOTS-c itself.
- Reynolds, Nat Commun 2021: in sedentary young men (n not obtained), exercise raised plasma MOTS-c ~1.5–1.6-fold and muscle ~12-fold. Endogenous-level observation, not a treatment trial. Mouse late-life treatment improved physical capacity. [ABSTRACT/SECONDARY]
- CB4211 (CohBar analogue; phase 1a 65 healthy adults; phase 1b 20 obese NAFLD, 4 wk): tolerable (injection-site reactions); ALT/AST and glucose fell; **liver fat −5.03% vs −4.88% placebo — no effect**; only a "trend" on weight. Sponsor press releases and a conference poster; no peer-reviewed paper found. [SECONDARY, sponsor COI]
- **Regulatory:** not approved anywhere. FDA PCAC voted 7–5 (2 abstentions) on 23 Jul 2026 to place MOTS-c on the 503A bulks list; FDA is not bound and rulemaking would follow; the panel had been re-composed beforehand with members reported to include peptide sellers (secondary; HHS disputes the framing). Public Citizen testified there are no published human studies. **This is a compounding vote, not evidence of efficacy.** TGA: not on ARTG. WADA: not found named; likely covered by S0 or S4.4 (metabolic modulators) — UNVERIFIED.
- Unknowns: everything clinical — efficacy, long-term safety, immunogenicity.

## Compounded and grey-market product

- **FDA, July 2024:** compounded semaglutide dosing errors, patients receiving 5–20x the intended dose, some hospitalised; causes include multi-dose vials, varying concentrations, unit confusion, prescriber miscalculation. Compounders used **semaglutide sodium/acetate salts**, which are different active ingredients from the approved base form. [SECONDARY]
- **Ashraf et al., JAMA Netw Open 2024** (doi 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.28280): test-bought from rogue no-prescription websites; prefilled-pen offers were all non-delivery scams demanding $650–1,200 "customs" fees; of 3 vials delivered, purity 7–14% vs 99% claimed, content 29–39% over label, one with elevated endotoxin. Tiny sample. [SECONDARY]
- A 2026 preprint on a public lab-testing dataset found purity score did not predict endotoxin. Not peer reviewed. [SECONDARY]
- **Australia:** GLP-1 RA compounding banned from 1 Oct 2024 (Therapeutic Goods Regulations amendment); ~20,000 patients were on compounded product. [SECONDARY]
- **Retatrutide:** see above — FDA warning letters, 14 hospitalisations in FAERS, Lilly litigation.
- **Industry COI:** all efficacy trials for the approved and pipeline incretins are sponsor-funded (Novo Nordisk, Lilly, Boehringer). Much of the online material on AOD-9604, MOTS-c and grey-market GLP-1s comes from peptide clinics, compounders and vendors with a direct sales interest; several pages surfaced in search were vendor sites and were not used as evidence.

## Relevance to lean, resistance-trained men

- **No trial enrolled them.** Every efficacy trial above required BMI ≥27–30. There are no RCTs of any of these agents in lean, trained people (searched; none found; LEAN-PREP NCT06885736 is in people with obesity).
- **Men lose less.** SURMOUNT-5 men lost ~6 points less than women in both arms. [SECONDARY]
- **Lean-mass cost.** In obese participants, 25–40% of loss is lean tissue. In a lean person the share of lean tissue in any weight lost is expected to be higher, because there is less fat to draw on — this is physiological inference, **not measured** for these drugs.
- **Resistance training is the only demonstrated mitigation** (17.5% vs ~26–31% lean share in meta-analysis), and it is in obese populations.
- **Practical consequences:** reduced appetite works against a surplus; GI AEs in most users; regain on stopping; WADA monitoring for sema/tirzepatide and probable S0 prohibition for retatrutide/survodutide/cagrilintide in tested sport. For the Muscle Band specifically: none of these is a performance enhancer for a lifter, and an obese user on one is losing lean tissue — the set-to-failure and volume tracking is relevant there, but claims about it must stay in fitness language.
