# One-day overeating ("binge") during a diet — research brief (registered 2026-11-08, before searching)

**Kye's question.** He is in a deficit 5 days a week, at maintenance on Friday and Saturday, and overeats by about
1,000 kcal on Sunday.
- Does that add fat, how much, and is it recovered within days?
- Why does he feel fatter on Monday while the scale reads the same?
- Is a binge "here and there" alright?

**Rules.** As `../agent-notes/SHARED-STANDARDS.md`:
- Never invent studies, numbers or DOIs.
- Mark access as FULL, ABSTRACT or SECONDARY.
- Separate acute overfeeding physiology from weeks-long fat change.
- Quantify where possible.
- Plain-English summary first.
- **Distinguish a planned high-calorie day from binge eating as a behaviour.** Loss-of-control eating can be a
  sign of binge eating disorder. Give a brief, non-alarmist note on when to seek help in Australia
  (e.g. Butterfly Foundation, GP). No diagnosis.
