# Fat loss & regional fat — research brief (registered 2026-11-04, before searching)

**Kye's model (to be TESTED, not assumed):**
1. Fat loss = a 200–300 kcal/day deficit.
2. Glucose/carbs are the body's #1 fuel; only once glucose/glycogen is "flattened out" does the body switch to body fat
   as fuel — which is why people do fasted cardio.
3. Lifters need carbs to fuel sessions; eat more carbs on training days and few carbs on rest days to be "flat" so the
   body burns fat (carb cycling).
4. Questions: can fat be lost from specific limbs (spot reduction)? Why do his arms carry a higher body-fat
   percentage than his belly or chest?

**Population:** resistance-trained men (Kye), intermediate–advanced, wanting fat loss while keeping muscle/strength.

**Standards** (as `../agent-notes/SHARED-STANDARDS.md`): never invent studies, numbers or DOIs; mark access (FULL /
ABSTRACT / SECONDARY); prefer RCTs, metabolic-ward/chamber studies and meta-analyses; separate acute substrate
oxidation (what fuel is burned in a session) from long-term fat-mass change (what actually matters); flag industry
funding and small samples; fact-check striking claims; say clearly where Kye's model is right, partly right or wrong,
and WHY (mechanism). Plain English summaries.

detail), `<ID>-studies.csv` with header
`key,topic,citation,doi_or_link,design,n,population,duration,intervention_comparison,outcome_measure,result_effect_uncertainty,limitations_coi,access,verification`
(quote all fields), `<ID>-searchlog.md`. Budget ~30 WebSearches each (shared pool). Try WebFetch on
